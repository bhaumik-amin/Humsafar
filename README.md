
# Humsafar

An AI family trip planner concept for Cleartrip mobile.
Made by Bhaumik Amin (M.Des Strategic Design Management, NID) for the Razorpay Design × NID assignment.

**Try it:** https://bhaumik-amin.github.io/Humsafar/

## What it shows
One journey for the Family Trip Planner persona: "Plan 5 days in Goa for 6 of us."
The agent asks only what it can't know, fits the plan to the budget, explains every pick,
and lets the parent change one piece at a time.

## How to explore
- Tap through the main journey on the phone.
- Use the list on the left (or the "States" button on a phone) to jump to edge cases:
  over budget, a room selling out mid-booking, a network drop.
- After booking, "Download trip PDF" gives the full trip pack.

## Notes
- This is a concept prototype. It is not affiliated with Cleartrip.
- AI replies, prices, timings and availability are simulated. Hotel names are fictional.
